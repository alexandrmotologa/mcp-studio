Refund Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

1. Our 14-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

We want you to be completely confident in your purchase of MCP Studio Pro. If MCP Studio does not meet your development expectations or is incompatible with your system, you are entitled to a 100% full refund within 14 days of your original purchase date.

2. Eligibility for a Refund

To qualify for a full refund:

  • Your refund request must be submitted within 14 calendar days from the transaction timestamp shown on your receipt.
  • You must have purchased your license key through our authorized Merchant of Record (Paddle.com) or official checkout link.

3. How to Request a Refund

Requesting a refund is simple and hassle-free:

  1. Send an email to alexander@mtlglabs.space or reply directly to your Paddle purchase confirmation receipt.
  2. Include your Paddle Order Number (e.g. #PAD-XXXXX) and the email address used during checkout.
  3. (Optional) Let us know the reason for your refund — your feedback helps us improve future releases!

4. Refund Processing Time

Once your request is received, our team or Paddle will process the refund within 24 to 48 hours. The refunded amount will be credited back to your original payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay) within 3 to 5 business days, depending on your card issuer or bank.

5. License Deactivation

Upon issuance of a refund, your Pro Lifetime license key will be automatically revoked and deactivated from our validation registry. You may continue to use the free Community Edition of MCP Studio at any time.
